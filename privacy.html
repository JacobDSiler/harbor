Harbor privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. This covers the Harbor: Family School Hub app (Android, iOS and harbor.jacobsiler.com).

Who we are

Harbor is made by Jacob Siler, trading as Siler Technology, Co. Westmeath, Ireland. For privacy questions or requests, email silertechireland@gmail.com.

What Harbor is for

Harbor is a hub for parents who homeschool. A parent links a family code so Harbor can show each child's progress from our learning apps (Math, Words, Electronics, Music and Sight Singing/Cantillo) and open those apps. Harbor is meant to be used by parents. Children use the learning apps, which a parent sets up.

What information is involved

  • Family code and settings (for example your Harbor PIN, which apps you hide, and how apps open) are kept on your own device.
  • Learner details you or your family enter: a child's first name or nickname, an avatar emoji, and school year or grade. Parents can add, edit or remove learners in Harbor.
  • Learning progress reported by the learning apps: quiz scores, topics practised, stars, badges, games played and last-active times.

Harbor does not ask for surnames, addresses, phone numbers, photos, precise location or payment details. It has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share information for marketing.

Where it is stored

Learner details and progress are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in a record identified by your family code. Google acts as our hosting and storage provider. Records are not tied to a name, email address or login. Anyone who has your family code can see that family's records, so please treat the code like a password and share it only with people you trust.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information directly from children, and children are not asked to create accounts or contact us. Learner details are entered and managed by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child's information was added without a parent's permission, contact us and we will remove it.

Microphone and permissions

Harbor itself does not record audio. The Sight Singing app opens inside Harbor or your browser and may ask to use the microphone to hear singing; it uses it only while you practise.

Your choices and deleting data

  • You can edit or remove any learner in the Harbor Parent area. Removing a learner removes them from the family record.
  • To have all data linked to your family code deleted, or to ask for a copy of it, email us from an address you control and include the family code. We aim to reply within 30 days.
  • Clearing Harbor's data on your device removes the family code and settings stored there.

If you are in the EU/UK you have rights to access, correct, delete and restrict use of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission).

Security and retention

Data is sent over HTTPS. We keep family records for as long as the family uses the apps, or until you ask us to delete them. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not enter anything you would not want a family member to see.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app.
